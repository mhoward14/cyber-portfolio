Grounded in NIST SP 800-61 (Computer Security Incident Handling Guide). Pick a scenario, make the calls a real responder would make, and get immediate feedback on whether each move was optimal.

This simulator presents original scenarios, graded against general incident-response principles from NIST SP 800-61 — it is not an official NIST assessment tool, and there is no single official answer key for real-world incident response. Use it to practice the reasoning, not as a certified rubric.
