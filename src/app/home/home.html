I believe security works best when it protects the mission and supports the people carrying it out. That perspective comes from 21 years of U.S. Air Force service in information technology, knowledge management, and security responsibilities, along with an M.S. in Cybersecurity & Information Assurance.

During my military career, I supported secure, regulated environments through COMSEC, security documentation, inspection preparation, and POA&M development and management. Those responsibilities taught me the value of clear guidance, accountability, and making security requirements work in everyday operations.

My graduate work built on that foundation through projects in cloud security, zero-trust architecture, penetration testing, risk assessment, and incident response. This portfolio brings that work together and shows how I approach security problems, evaluate risks, and develop practical solutions. I'm particularly interested in connecting governance and risk management with the technical work of designing and securing systems.
