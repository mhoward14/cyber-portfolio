Packet Analysis Lab

A Wireshark-style workbench in the browser. Pick a threat hunt or open your own capture, filter it with display filters, inspect each packet down to the byte, and answer the questions an analyst would have to answer.

The hunts' captures are synthetic, generated byte-for-byte in your browser from fictional hosts: internal addresses in 10.0.0.0/8, external addresses from the RFC 5737 documentation ranges, and names under the reserved .example domain. Captures you open yourself are decoded in your browser and never uploaded. The filter bar understands a subset of Wireshark's display-filter syntax, so filters you learn here work in Wireshark too.
