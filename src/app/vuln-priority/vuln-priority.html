// HOW THE ORDER IS DECIDED

The CVSS rating gives 0 to 4 points (none, low, medium, high, critical). Exploitation in the wild adds 2 and public exploit code adds 1. Internet exposure adds 1. A business-critical system adds 1 and a low-importance one takes away 1. Seven or more points is P1, five or six is P2, three or four is P3 and anything lower is P4. A finding with no impact is always P4. The target days (7, 30, 90 and 180) are examples; a real program sets them in policy.

This is a teaching rule, not an official standard, so every point is shown beside the result and you can disagree with a specific one. CVSS scores follow the FIRST CVSS v3.1 specification, and the calculator is checked against its published example scores. Findings are tracked under NIST SP 800-53 RA-5 (vulnerability monitoring and scanning) and SI-2 (flaw remediation).
